Eternity

Eternity exists and all things in eternity are independent of your creative act. You can go on building only on what your mortal eye sees, and perpetuate the same thing over and over again, forever remaining where you are.

But if you know that all things exist, although invisible for the moment, and that you have access to them through your imagination, you can rearrange the pattern of your thinking and change your world while remaining faithful to your invisible construction.

And when it is externalized by becoming a fact that you can share with others, then you will have found the secret of creation, which was an act of faith.
