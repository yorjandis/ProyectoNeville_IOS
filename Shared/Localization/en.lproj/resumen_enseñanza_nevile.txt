
Summary of the principles in Neville Goddard’s teachings. Each principle also presents a point of convergence with the teachings of Dr. Joe Dispenza.

🟢 Consciousness is the only reality

Goddard
There is no external cause. Everything that appears in the world is an effect of the state of consciousness you occupy.

🔶 Similarity to Joe Dispenza: Deep convergence
    • Dispenza: Reality reflects the dominant mental and emotional state.
    • Goddard: The world is a mirror of consciousness.

    👉 Nothing changes outside until something changes within.



🟢 Imagination is the creative power

Goddard
Imagination is not fantasy: it is the creative faculty of consciousness. Everything that exists was first imagined and accepted.

🔶 Similarity to Joe Dispenza
    • Dispenza: The brain does not distinguish between a real experience and one that is vividly imagined.
    • Goddard: To imagine with acceptance is to create.

    👉 Conscious imagination is a rehearsal of future reality.



🟢 Feeling is the secret

Goddard
Manifestation occurs when you feel the reality of the wish fulfilled. Feeling is the creative signal.

🔶 Similarity to Joe Dispenza
    • Dispenza: An elevated emotion is the signal that reorganizes the energy field.
    • Goddard: Feeling impresses the image upon consciousness.

    👉 Emotion is the language of creation.



🟢 Living from the end

Goddard
You must inwardly occupy the state in which the desire is already a fact, rather than wait for it to happen.

🔶 Similarity to Joe Dispenza
    • Dispenza: Act as though the event has already occurred.
    • Goddard: Assume the state of the wish fulfilled.

    👉 Being precedes having.



🟢 “I Am” defines identity

Goddard
“I Am” is consciousness itself. Whatever you declare after those words becomes your identity.

🔶 Similarity to Joe Dispenza
    • Dispenza: Personality creates personal reality.
    • Goddard: “I Am” is the cause of every experience.

    👉 Change your identity and you change your life.



🟢 There are no people or circumstances, only states

Goddard
Circumstances are not causes; they are symptoms of an inner state. Change the state and everything reorganizes itself.

🔶 Similarity to Joe Dispenza
    • Dispenza: Do not struggle against the environment; change your inner state.
    • Goddard: Do not try to change the world; change yourself.

    👉 The world adjusts to the state you occupy.



🟢 Persistence in the new state

Goddard
The key is not an isolated act, but remaining faithful to the new state until it is externalized.

🔶 Similarity to Joe Dispenza
    • Dispenza: Repeat the inner experience until the body learns it.
    • Goddard: Persist in the assumption.

    👉 Repetition consolidates the new reality.



🟢 Falling asleep in the assumption

Goddard
The state between wakefulness and sleep is ideal for impressing consciousness because the critical mind grows quiet.

🔶 Similarity to Joe Dispenza
    • Dispenza: Deep meditative states make reprogramming possible.
    • Goddard: Reaching a state close to sleep makes it easier to plant the new state.

    👉 A relaxed mind is a creative mind.



🟢 Letting go of the old self

Goddard
To assume a new state, the old one must die psychologically. Nothing is added; something is released.

🔶 Similarity to Joe Dispenza: Deep convergence
    • Dispenza: Become “no one” to make change possible.
    • Goddard: Abandon the old state in order to assume a new one.

    👉 Nothing new is added: an obsolete identity is released.



🟢 Everything is God expressing itself as consciousness

Goddard
God is not external: God is individualized consciousness experiencing itself.

🔶 Similarity to Joe Dispenza
    • Dispenza: The individual mind is an extension of universal intelligence.
    • Goddard: You and God are one in essence.

    👉 The creator is not separate from the creation.
