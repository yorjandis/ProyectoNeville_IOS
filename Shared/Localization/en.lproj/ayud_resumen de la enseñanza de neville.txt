Summary of Neville's teachings:

Consciousness is the only reality and the cause of all experience in life. Everything we experience is a reflection of our inner state, since the external world acts as a mirror of what we believe and feel ourselves to be. What is accepted as truth in the mind and felt intensely materializes in the objective world. Therefore, we do not attract what we desire, but what we believe and feel to be true in the present.

Desire must be assumed as an accomplished fact in order for it to manifest. Asking or waiting is equivalent to acknowledging its absence, while feeling that we already possess it activates the creative power of the imagination and the subconscious. True prayer consists of assuming the feeling of being or having what we desire, until it feels natural and real. The impressions that are imprinted on the subconscious through feelings determine the conditions of our lives.

Change in external experience requires a change in one's self-conception. We should not try to change the world or other people, because they are merely messengers who reflect our self-concept. Raising consciousness to the level of the fulfilled desire and remaining in that state causes circumstances to transform in harmony with this new internal pattern.

Sleep and states of relaxation are key moments for the creation of subjective states and experiences. Before sleeping, it is essential to embrace the feeling of the fulfilled desire, since the subconscious works during rest and will express in the physical world what has been imprinted upon it. Every emotional reaction, positive or negative, imprints a pattern on the subconscious that will manifest as a future experience.

Visible manifestation arises from persistent imagination and the sustained feeling of having achieved the desired outcome. Shifting attention from current limitations to the ideal state, feeling it as one's own, and maintaining it inevitably leads to its externalization. Faith, understood as a feeling of present reality, is the means by which all creation becomes tangible.
