The Art of Revision in Neville's Words

Introduction (by the editor):

Imagined actions, emotional reactions, and the content of our inner speech are not moving backward into the past, but rather forward to create future events and experiences. It is vital to keep this point in mind, as it is crucial for understanding the importance of using the art of revision to detect and change past actions that are causing the events of the present and tomorrow.

The Technique:

At the end of the day, take a moment to review every event and reaction. If something unpleasant happened, DON'T LET IT STAY, REWRITE IT! Take that same scene and rewrite it, and having rewritten it, REPLAY IT until it takes on the tones of reality (you will feel relief and positive emotion, and it's done). In your imagination, picture the (rewritten) action unfolding and replay it as you should have done it the first time. Time is never going backward as people think; time is moving forward. Yesterday is today's future due to the curvature of time.

Source: Lecture "The Coin of Heaven" (Neville - 1954)
