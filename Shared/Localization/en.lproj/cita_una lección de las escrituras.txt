Quote from Conference A LESSON FROM THE SCRIPTURES 10/23/1967
Do you want to be rich? That is the story of Jesus, which is a persistent assumption in the conviction that “I am rich”, because if you do not believe that “I am rich” you die in your sins and continue affirming “I am poor”. Do you want to be known? Then he persistently assumes: “I am known.” Do you want to be healthy? “I am healthy!” Regardless of what you want to be, you have to declare that you already are and persist in that assumption.

An assumption is an act of faith, and without faith it is impossible to please God. Your rational mind can deny wealth. Your senses may also deny it, but if you have faith you will dare to take on wealth, thus becoming the man you want to be. ~Neville Goddard
