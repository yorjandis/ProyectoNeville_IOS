Quote from the TRUST IN GOD Conference 10/13/1967
Put your trust in the name of God. Knowing what you want, believe that your assumption will turn it into a fact. Believe that you do not need anyone on the outside to help you, for all things are possible with God. Assume that things are the way you want them to be, for an assumption, if persisted in, will solidify into fact! ~Neville Goddard
