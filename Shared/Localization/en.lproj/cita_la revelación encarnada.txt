REVELATION INCARNATE - Lecture #89 - Neville 02/20/1969
Assume

Decide what you want and ask yourself what it would be like and how it would feel if it were true. Then dare to assume that you have it. Let the people who know you see you now after your inauguration. Don't make them see you; let them see the change!

Think of the world as if it were a sounding board, echoing and reflecting what you have assumed. Listen to your friends comment on your change. See their faces expressing joy at your good fortune. Carry that feeling as you carry your current body of belief now. Continue carrying that new state and in less than nothing your desire will be objectified and become a fact in your world. Then you will know who is really the cause of the phenomena of life.
