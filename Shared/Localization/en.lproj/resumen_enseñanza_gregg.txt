
Summary of the principles in Gregg Braden’s teachings. Each principle also presents a point of convergence with the teachings of Neville Goddard.

🟢 Human consciousness directly influences physical reality

Gregg Braden teaches that reality is not fixed: it responds to human consciousness, emotion, and perception.

🔶 Similarity to Neville Goddard: Deep convergence
    • Gregg: The physical world responds to the field of consciousness and the emotional information we emit.
    • Neville: The world is a mirror of the state of consciousness inwardly assumed.

    👉 The world is not changed; the inner state from which we observe it is changed.



🟢 Feeling is the language that moves creation

For Braden, felt emotion is the link connecting thought and matter.

🔶 Similarity to Neville Goddard: Essential convergence
    • Gregg: Coherent emotion is the signal that reorganizes the quantum field.
    • Neville: The feeling of the wish fulfilled is the key to manifestation.

    👉 Thinking differently is not enough; we must feel from the fulfilled identity.



🟢 We live immersed in an intelligent, unified field

Braden speaks of the divine field, matrix, or quantum field: a living intelligence that connects everything.

🔶 Similarity to Neville Goddard: Ontological convergence
    • Gregg: A conscious field exists that responds to human intention.
    • Neville: God is the individual’s own consciousness, operating as law.

    👉 We are not separate from the field; we are the field operating locally.



🟢 The heart is a center of creative intelligence

Gregg emphasizes heart–brain coherence as the optimal state for creating reality.

🔶 Similarity to Neville Goddard: Functional convergence
    • Gregg: The heart generates an electromagnetic field that influences reality.
    • Neville: Deep feeling—the heart—precedes every external manifestation.

    👉 The mind imagines, but the heart confirms reality.



🟢 Reality responds to identity, not desire

Braden insists that the universe responds to who we believe we are, not to what we ask for.

🔶 Similarity to Neville Goddard: Complete convergence
    • Gregg: Creation occurs when we embody the identity that is coherent with the outcome.
    • Neville: “Assume the feeling of the wish fulfilled, and it will be expressed.”

    👉 Nothing new is added: an obsolete identity is released.



🟢 The past does not determine the present

For Braden, the past is information, not destiny; it can be reinterpreted from the present.

🔶 Similarity to Neville Goddard: Methodological convergence
    • Gregg: Changing our perception of the past reconfigures the present field.
    • Neville: Revising the past changes its effects in the present.

    👉 Time does not bind us; consciousness redefines the story.



🟢 The human being is a bridge between science and spirituality

Gregg Braden integrates ancient texts with modern physics, revealing a shared truth.

🔶 Similarity to Neville Goddard: Philosophical convergence
    • Gregg: Modern science validates what mystical traditions taught.
    • Neville: The Bible is a psychological and metaphysical map, not a historical one.

    👉 Truth is not new; it is simply remembered in another language.



🟢 Prayer is not pleading; it is felt certainty

Braden redefines prayer as an assumed emotional state, not a request for the future.

🔶 Similarity to Neville Goddard: Practical convergence
    • Gregg: Effective prayer occurs when we already feel the outcome as real.
    • Neville: To pray is to enter the state of the wish already fulfilled.

    👉 We do not ask; we affirm from the inner experience.



🟢 Separation is a learned illusion

Gregg teaches that the sense of separation produces fear, conflict, and illness.

🔶 Similarity to Neville Goddard: Metaphysical convergence
    • Gregg: Disconnection from the field produces suffering.
    • Neville: Belief in an “other” separate from oneself is the root of error.

    👉 Healing is remembering unity, not repairing something broken.



🟢 Human evolution is a shift in consciousness, not technology

For Braden, the true evolutionary leap is inward, rooted in identity and consciousness.

🔶 Similarity to Neville Goddard: Final convergence
    • Gregg: The future depends on the level of consciousness we embody today.
    • Neville: The awakened person governs their world through conscious imagination.

    👉 Evolution does not add abilities; it reveals what was always latent within us.



Gregg Braden and Neville Goddard converge on one essential point:
☘️ Reality is not changed from the outside; it is automatically expressed from the identity we assume.
