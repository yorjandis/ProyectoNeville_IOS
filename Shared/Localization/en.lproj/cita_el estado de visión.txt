THE STATE OF VISION - Lecture #32 - Neville 02/26/1968
Sense

Begin now to exercise your God-given talents of sight, hearing, touch, taste and smell – together or separately. The sense of touch is the strongest in me.

If someone expresses his wish to me in a letter, I touch the paper he wrote, believing that it tells me about the realization of his wish. I know that only one in ten responds after I have granted their request, but that's okay.

Having felt their letter and seen its contents [in my imagination], I know that I have imbued myself with the good luck they desire, and in doing so, my own captivity is lifted.
